## Grades changed

None. Eight rows, still 3 `solid` / 2 `mixed` / 3 `folklore`.

- `chain-of-thought` stays `mixed`. What moved is the scope, not the grade: Anthropic ([Prompting Claude Opus 5.5](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/prompting-claude-opus-5-5)) and Google ([Gemini 3 developer guide](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/gemini-3)) now say what OpenAI has said since the o-series: on a model with a thinking control, adjust the control rather than prompt for step-by-step reasoning. Recorded as dated `when_not` bullets.
- `self-consistency` stays `solid`. Cai et al. 2026 ([arXiv:2605.26172](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26172)) describe a wrong-majority failure mode on two small open-weight models; the consistent gains the grade rests on are not contradicted at the scale they were measured. Recorded as a dated `when_not` bullet.
- `data-before-instruction` stays `solid`. Veseli et al. 2025 ([arXiv:2508.07479](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.07479)) qualify the mechanism (lost-in-the-middle is strongest below about 50 percent context utilisation), not the advice (query last, which a stable recency bias supports).
